📱 Install InstantID

Get the full app experience on your phone

🍎 iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open this page in Safari (not Chrome)
  2. Tap the Share button (square with arrow up)
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
  4. Tap "Add" in the top-right corner
  5. Find the InstantID icon on your home screen
💡 Tip: After adding, open the app from your home screen — it will run full-screen without the Safari bar.

🤖 Android (Chrome)

  1. Open this page in Chrome
  2. Tap the three dots in the top-right corner
  3. Tap "Install app" or "Add to Home Screen"
  4. Tap "Install"
  5. Find the InstantID icon on your home screen
💡 Tip: Chrome usually shows an "Install app" banner automatically when you visit — tap it to install in one tap.

💻 Desktop (Chrome / Edge)

  1. Open this page in Chrome or Edge
  2. Look at the address bar — you'll see an install icon (monitor with a down arrow)
  3. Click it and select "Install"
  4. InstantID will open in its own window, without the browser bar
💡 Tip: Or click the three dots → "Install page as app"
